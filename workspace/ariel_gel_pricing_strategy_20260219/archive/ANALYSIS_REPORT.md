In May 2025, Ariel raised the regular bottle (本体通常) shelf price by +29%. In the exact same month, Attack cut its regular bottle price by -22%. The shelf gap more than doubled overnight.

## Methodology (Brief)

1. **Price landscape** (NB01): Calculated average transaction price per brand per size per week. Indexed pre- vs post-May 2025 renewal. Computed price gap between Ariel and Attack as the primary market share predictor.

2. **Trial acquisition** (NB02): Identified first-time buyers for each brand/size combination. Measured price-buyer relationship by tracking new buyer counts against weekly average price. Tested 100 store-weeks per size to determine promotional effectiveness.

3. **Repeat measurement** (NB03): Tracked trial buyers forward 6 months to measure return rate. Compared pre- vs post-renewal cohorts to measure product improvement impact.

4. **Lapse analysis** (NB04): Identified buyers who purchased Ariel in the first half of the period and did not return. Tracked their destinations: Attack (by size), other brands, or category exit. Compared price paid by lapsed vs retained buyers to find tipping points.

5. **Buyer flow** (NB05): Mapped the full pipeline from first purchase size through retention/loss to destination. Calculated 3-year LTV for loyal buyers at each size to quantify the cost of losing buyers at each pipeline stage.

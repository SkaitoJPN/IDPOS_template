## Executive Summary: The Battle Is Won or Lost at the Front Door

**¥2.58 billion** in annual Ariel Gel revenue is at risk. **1.78 million shoppers** stopped buying. But the strategic error in V1 was misdiagnosing the root cause.

**V1 said:** "Shoppers are switching to the same Attack size at large refills — we need to defend every size head-to-head."

**V2 says:** "Our loyalty fortress at large refills is strong but starving for new entrants. The battle is at the front door (本体通常)."

**The evidence:**

- 本体通常 has the **strongest price sensitivity** of any size (r=-0.554) — price directly controls trial volume _(See NB02 Cell 20)_
- 詰替超ｳﾙﾄﾗｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ has **ZERO price sensitivity** (r=+0.107) — price cuts on large refills don't bring new buyers _(See NB02 Cell 20)_
- Ariel's repeat advantage is **+12-17pp over Attack on ALL refill sizes** — once buyers enter refills, they stay _(See NB03 Cell 10)_
- 本体通常 lapse rate is **54.9%** — worst of all sizes — this is where the funnel breaks _(See NB04 Cell 11)_
- Same-size switching at large refills (95% on Mega Jumbo) is a **SYMPTOM** of the entry problem, not the cause _(See NB04 Cell 18)_

**The cascade:** Fewer trial shoppers at 本体通常 → fewer candidates for 詰替超特大 size-up → fewer pipeline into loyalty sizes → the refill loyalty fortress (92% same-pack repeat on Mega Jumbo) becomes irrelevant because nobody enters it.

**Three things must happen — in this order of priority:**

1. **Open the front door wider** — 本体通常 everyday shelf price ≤¥350, ¥200-250 trial promotions, paired with refill upgrade offers
2. **Protect the bridge** — 詰替超特大 ≤¥450 upgrade offer, weekly ¥50 gap trigger vs Attack
3. **Hold the fortress** — 詰替ﾒｶﾞｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ ≤¥950, 詰替ｳﾙﾄﾗｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ ≤¥800

**Cost of 90-day delay:** ~252,000 buyers + ~¥239M in revenue permanently transferred to Attack.

### The Front Door Narrative

**What happened:** In May 2025, Ariel raised the regular bottle (本体通常) price by +29%. At the same time, Attack cut the same size by -22%. The shelf price gap more than doubled overnight. _(See NB01 Cell 11 — combined Ariel vs Attack ASP monthly trend; See NB01 Cell 12 — unit ratio drops as ASP gap widens)_

**Why 本体通常 is the strategic bottleneck — not large refills:**

本体通常 is the entry point. It is the first Ariel Gel product a new buyer touches. When this price goes up, the entire downstream pipeline starves:

- **本体通常 has 563,730 trial shoppers** — the largest trial pool. And it is the ONLY size where Ariel leads Attack (Index 116 vs <100 on all refills). _(See NB02 Cell 15)_
- **Price sensitivity is strongest here** — r=-0.554. Most productive band: ¥150-199 (trial rate peaks at 44.88%). _(See NB02 Cell 20)_
- **Lapse rate is worst here** — 54.9% vs Attack's 42.6% on the same size. 406,262 buyers lost. _(See NB04 Cell 11)_
- **Lapsed buyers paid only ¥255** at last purchase vs ¥408 for stayers — the ¥198 promo recruited price-sensitive buyers who couldn't sustain at regular price. _(See NB03 Cell 9)_

**The downstream consequence:** Each buyer who leaves 本体通常 is one fewer candidate for the 詰替超特大 bridge → one fewer candidate for 詰替ｳﾙﾄﾗｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ/詰替超ｳﾙﾄﾗｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ → one fewer candidate for the Mega Jumbo loyalty fortress. Our 12-17pp repeat advantage on refills _(See NB03 Cell 10)_ and our 92% same-pack repeat on Mega Jumbo become irrelevant if the front door is too narrow.

**Key visual evidence:**

- **Price gap doubled → unit ratio dropped:** See NB01 Cell 12. When Ariel became expensive vs Attack, the unit ratio (Ariel÷Attack) immediately declined. This is the smoking gun.
- **Trial shoppers per month:** See NB02 Cell 14. Combined monthly trial Ariel vs Attack — the teal bars show trial declining post-May 2025.
- **Lapse rate + ASP band:** See NB04 Cell 11. 本体通常's 54.9% is the red flag. Compare to 詰替ｳﾙﾄﾗｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ at 39.3% (best) — the problem is clearly at entry, not at loyalty sizes.

### Why V1 Was Wrong About Same-Size Switching

V1 emphasized: _"All shoppers move toward competitors' same size — 95% of Mega Jumbo leavers go to Attack Mega Jumbo."_

The user correctly challenged this: **"Do we really care about Attack's 詰替超ｳﾙﾄﾗｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ or 詰替ﾒｶﾞｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ price point?"**

**The answer: only partially.** Here is the reframed logic:

**Same-size switching on large refills is REAL but it is a SYMPTOM, not the CAUSE.**

| 92% same-pack repeat on Mega Jumbo                  | The fortress is strong — it just needs more entrants        | See NB03 Cell 14 |

**The correct narrative:**

> "The battle is won or lost at the front door (本体通常). Our loyalty fortress at large refills is strong but starving for new entrants."

**What same-size switching actually tells us:**

- 95% same-size switching on Mega Jumbo _(See NB04 Cell 18)_ → these shoppers were loyal UNTIL price crossed ¥950. This is a **price ceiling enforcement** problem, not a competitive pricing problem. Action: hold ≤¥950.
- 73% same-size switching on 詰替超ｳﾙﾄﾗｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ _(See NB04 Cell 18)_ → but r=+0.107 means **price cuts will NOT win them back**. Action: invest in shelf presence, not discounts.
- 45% same-size switching on 詰替超特大 _(See NB04 Cell 18)_ → this IS a genuine competitive battleground because it's the bridge size. Action: defend the ¥50 price gap.

**Where to focus resources:** Fix the front door (本体通常 pricing), protect the bridge (詰替超特大 gap), and hold the fortress ceiling (詰替ﾒｶﾞｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ ≤¥950). Do NOT invest in price cuts on 詰替超ｳﾙﾄﾗｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ — the data says they don't work.

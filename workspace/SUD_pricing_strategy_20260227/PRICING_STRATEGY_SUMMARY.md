## 7. Strategic Recommendations

### 7a. ハイパージャンボ Pricing Strategy

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  RECOMMENDED: Target HJ shelf price of ¥848 – ¥898             │
│                                                                 │
│  Bold:  ¥898 (maximize retention at 39.3% repeat)              │
│  Ariel: ¥848 (maximize size-up at 23.6% while repeat=38.2%)   │
│                                                                 │
│  OR both at ¥898 if retention is the primary KPI               │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Rationale for brand differentiation:**

- **Bold** has stronger trial (251K vs 185K 本体通常) and higher base repeat — it can afford to price HJ at the upper end (¥898) to maximize per-shopper retention.
- **Ariel** has a net shopper loss to Bold (−15,707) — pricing HJ at ¥848 makes it more competitive, drives the highest size-up rate (23.6%), and narrows the retention gap.

### 7b. Broader Size Strategy

| Size                | Strategic Action                                                                                     | Price Implication                                                      |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **本体通常**        | **Acquire aggressively** — this is the entry door. Accept low repeat (27-34%) as cost of acquisition | Keep trial ASP competitive (~¥200-250); subsidize trial via promotions |
| **詰替ﾊｲﾊﾟｰｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ** | **Optimize for retention + size-up** — the critical bridge size                                      | **¥848-898** (see above)                                               |
| **詰替ﾒｶﾞｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ**   | **MJ is a "pivot" size** — high churn, high mobility in both directions                              | Maintain clear value vs 超MJ; keep gap to HJ at ~¥900-1,000            |
| **詰替超ﾒｶﾞｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ** | **Loyalty stronghold** — highest same-size retention (Ariel 61.2%, Bold 53.8%)                       | Price to reward loyalty; avoid eroding per-unit value                  |
| **詰替ﾃﾗｼﾞｬﾝﾎﾞ**    | **"Ultimate loyal"** — smallest base but ~75% same-size retention                                    | Premium pricing sustainable; very sticky once acquired                 |

### 7c. Price Ladder Principles

1. **HJ → MJ gap should be ~¥900-1,050** — too narrow (¥900) and there's no incentive to try MJ first at HJ; too wide (¥1,100+) and shoppers won't step up
2. **本体通常 → HJ gap should be ~¥600-650** — make the jump accessible from trial entry
3. **Avoid ¥950+ for HJ** — category exit rises above 60%, destroying the loyalty pipeline
4. **Avoid ¥798 or below for HJ** — repeat rate collapses to 28-31%, wasting acquired shoppers
